Flip

An eco-friendly game by the Flip Team

About the Game

Flip! is a two‑sided board experience. Flip the plate to change the game:

  • Side A: Four‑player chess with a twist.
  • Side B: The Crazy Labyrinth, our own wild maze variant.

All components are produced through eco‑friendly methods – from recycled boards to plant‑based inks.

Features

  • Dual‑mode board (chess + labyrinth)
  • Play solo or with friends
  • Eco‑friendly materials & production
  • Bright, dynamic design and glow effects
